# OpenFolio Docs

OpenFolio privately searches your iMessage history on your Mac, helps you find
the person, fact, or message you remember, and lets you verify it in the
original conversation.

**OpenFolio remembers who told you what.**

## Product shape

- **Search** is the default and primary workflow.
- **People** organizes the archive by person.
- **Conversations** preserves the original evidence and surrounding context.
- **Wrapped** is a deterministic annual reflection, not a generated story.
- `apps/mac` is the product, `packages/core` is its canonical local behavior
  layer, and `apps/web` contains this public website and documentation.

OpenFolio is not a relationship-health score, CRM, reminder system, generated
answer product, hosted message backup, Obsidian product, or MCP product.
